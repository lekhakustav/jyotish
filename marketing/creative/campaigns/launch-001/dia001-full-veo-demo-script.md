## Why the previous edit failed

The rejected eight-second edit treated the Veo scene and app proof as interchangeable fragments.
They are not.

1. The Veo source is a complete miniature story. Sydney occupies seconds 0–3.8 and Kathmandu
   occupies seconds 3.8–8.0. Cutting away at second 2.3 removed the match cut and therefore
   removed the emotional meaning of "two time zones."
2. Returning for an unrelated 0.7-second mother shot did not restore that meaning. It read as an
   arbitrary interruption.
3. The proof section was made from static screenshots held for roughly two seconds each. It did
   not show a user action, a transition, or a result, so it was not a product demonstration.
4. The oversized dark hook card competed with the cup, hand, phone, and face even though the Veo
   prompt deliberately reserved quiet upper-center space for light editorial text.
5. The QR cover became the visual subject. Privacy masking should quietly remove the payload,
   not replace the product with a large editorial graphic.
6. The eight-second ceiling left no time to understand the sender action, receiver choice, and
   saved result. The concept needs the full Veo story followed by a legible native demo.

## Narrative logic

The daughter is in Sydney. Her mother, Sita Sharma, is in Kathmandu. The full Veo clip establishes
their distance and connection through the matched tea-cup action. The native demo then answers a
single practical question:

> How does Sita deliberately give her daughter the family context to keep on the other phone?

The proof must show all three truthful steps:

1. Sita opens **Share My Kundli** and sees **My Private Kundli QR**.
2. Her daughter scans the code and deliberately chooses **Mother**.
3. The receiving phone saves **Sita Sharma — Mother** in **My Kundli & QR**.

This is a chosen handoff, not account syncing. The receiver chooses the relationship. The QR
payload is sensitive and never appears in an exported frame.

## Spoken script

Delivery is intimate, plain, and unhurried. Do not use a dramatic astrology voice.

> One family. Two time zones.
>
> Distance should not mean losing family context.
>
> In Jyotish Baje, Sita shares her birth profile through a private Kundli QR.
>
> On the other phone, her daughter scans it, chooses “Mother,” and saves Sita to the family.
>
> Birth data is sensitive. Share it only with someone you trust.

The brand is spoken once in the demo sentence. The final CTA is visual rather than another spoken
sales line.

## Text rules

- The hook and final CTA are the only promotional text.
- Do not add numbered steps, floating labels, feature badges, a headline card, or a second
  explanation over native UI.
- The hook uses the existing quiet upper-center area. It must not cover a hand, cup, phone, or
  face.
- Do not write `PRIVATE QR` over the code. Use only a small lock symbol inside the full-region
  privacy mask.
- Keep the final CTA on top of the proven result instead of cutting away to a generic end card.

## Editing rules

- Use all 192 frames of the Veo source in their original order.
- Preserve source cadence at 24 fps and conform native captures without changing action speed.
- No browser recording, synthetic scrolling, fake taps, cursor, fake scan beam, or generated app
  UI.
- Do not cut during a tap, sheet transition, picker transition, or save dismissal.
- For every native action, show the state before the action, the real transition, and the result.
- The only internal edit in the demo is the clearly motivated sender-device to receiver-device
  transition.
- Keep the Veo room tone for the complete first eight seconds. Any later music bed must stay
  below the voice and native feedback; no ringtone, temple bell, or cosmic sound design.

## Capture requirements before rendering

1. Sender recording: Sita Sharma fixture, **My Kundli & QR** → **Share My Kundli** → settled
   **My Private Kundli QR** sheet.
2. Receiver recording on a second clean physical device: settled **Scan Kundli QR** → real
   scan/decode → decoded **Sita Sharma** import form → choose **Mother** → **Add to My Kundli**.
3. Result recording: real post-save family screen with **Sita Sharma — Mother** visible for at
   least three clean seconds.
4. The raw QR may exist only in restricted source capture. The entire region must be covered in

5. Receiver account identity, notifications, encoded text, birth values, and every other person's
   name remain outside the final crop.
