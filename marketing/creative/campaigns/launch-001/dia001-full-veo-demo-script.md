## The three-line script

The narration is deliberately tagline-like. It does not describe every tap.

> One family. Two time zones.
>
> Distance should not mean losing family context.
>
> Keep your family’s astrology close with Jyotish Baje.

The third line states the product value without turning the video into a feature tutorial. The
native recording supplies the proof.

## Background sound design

### Veo story, 0–8 seconds

- Preserve the actual cup, fabric, kitchen, and room ambience from the complete Veo file.
- Begin with natural room tone only. Let the first cup placement feel tactile.
- Bring in a soft modern acoustic motif around second 1: muted plucked strings, a low warm pad,
  and no percussion at first.
- Let the source ambience carry the Sydney-to-Kathmandu match cut at second 3.8. Do not add a
  whoosh or transition hit.
- After the match cut, gently open the same musical motif rather than introducing a new theme.

### Native demo, 8–24 seconds

- Continue the warm music bed at low volume so the move into the app feels like the same story.
- Use very soft tactile tap ticks only where a real button press occurs.
- Do not add a scanner beep, success chime, notification, ringtone, or sound that the shipped app
  does not make.
- At the saved Sita result, let the music resolve into one warm sustained chord. Do not create a
  dramatic reveal.
- Keep narration centered and close. Duck the music slightly under each of the three lines, then
  restore the room/music bed during the pauses.

### Sounds that do not belong

No temple bells, chant, cosmic sparkle, magical shimmer, ominous bass, wedding music, sentimental
guilt cue, or stereotyped “mystical India” instrumentation.

## Native copy gate

The current family-sharing screens contain terminology the user rejected. They cannot be used
unchanged in this ad. Before capture, the visible English labels need a neutral family-profile
vocabulary:

| Current function | Capture-facing label |
| --- | --- |
| Family hub | `My Family` |
| Sender action | `Share My Birth Profile` |
| Private sender sheet | `My Private Birth Profile` |
| Receiver action | `Scan Family Profile` |
| Save action | `Add to Family` |
| Saved result | `Sita Sharma — Mother` |

This is a real product-copy decision, not an editorial crop trick. Do not render the family demo
until the native labels are updated or another real shipped path proves the same value without
the rejected terminology.

- this three-line script and sound plan are accepted;
- the native family-profile labels are approved;
- continuous sender and receiver recordings exist;
- only Sita Sharma is readable;
- the private code is covered in every exported frame; and
- the final result remains visible long enough to understand without narration.
