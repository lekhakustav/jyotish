# Image requirements

Prepare one profile picture and one Facebook cover image. The profile picture should be reused on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

## Profile picture

- **Master size:** 1080 x 1080 px
- **File type:** PNG
- **Quality:** High quality
- **Composition:** Circular and safe for circular cropping
- **Main subject:** A friendly Jyotish Baje character or simple symbol
- **Details:** Small tilak and one sun, moon or zodiac-inspired element
- **Style:** Nepali traditional or spiritual appearance with a modern, trustworthy finish
- **Background:** Clean light background
- **Text:** No small text; ideally no text inside the small profile image
- **Colors:** Maximum three main colors
- **Recommended palette:** Cream, saffron, deep red and/or dark blue
- **Small-size check:** The face or symbol must remain clear when shown very small

Keep the main face or symbol inside the central safe circle. Leave comfortable space around it so no important part is cut off.

## Facebook cover photo

- **Recommended size:** 1640 x 624 px
- **Text:** `राशिफलदेखि कुण्डलीसम्म — सबै ज्योतिष जानकारी एउटै एपमा`
- **Include:** Jyotish Baje logo, a sun/moon/zodiac element and a simple Nepali cultural touch
- **Layout:** Clean, with large readable text and space reserved for the official app or website link
- **Avoid:** Unnecessary details, crowded decoration and very small writing

Keep important text and the logo away from the far edges so Facebook cropping does not hide them on phones.

## Export check

Before uploading, check that:

- the profile picture is a sharp PNG;
- the profile picture works inside a circle;
- the cover text can be read on a phone;
- no password, OTP or private information appears in either image.

This file is a design specification only. It does not create or upload images.
