## Exact manual setup order

1. Open Facebook, Instagram and TikTok yourself.
2. Register each account manually with `lekhakutsav@gmail.com`.
3. Choose and confirm the first available username from the order above.
4. Verify the email address when the platform asks.
5. Set the display name, category, bio, location, profile picture and contact email.
6. Create the Facebook Page and add the About text, Intro, cover photo and Learn More button.
7. Change Instagram to a professional account, choose Digital Creator or App, and turn on the email contact button.
8. Set up TikTok as a Creator or Business account if suitable, then connect Instagram if the option is available.
9. Connect Facebook and Instagram through Meta Business Suite.
10. Confirm that messages from both platforms can be seen in Meta Business Suite.
11. Complete the security checklist in [security-checklist.md](security-checklist.md).
